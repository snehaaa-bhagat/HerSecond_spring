Nutrition

What you eat can ease symptoms and support your body through hormonal changes.

  • Calcium-rich foods(dairy, leafy greens) to protect bone density
  • Phytoestrogen sources like soy and flaxseed may help balance hormones
  • Limiting caffeine and alcohol can reduce hot flashes for some women

Exercise

moving your body is must,few exercises which may help.

  • Walking or low impact cardio helps manage weight changes and supports heart health
  • Strength training helps counter bone density loss during menopause
  • Yoga or stretching can ease joint stiffness and improve sleep

Symptoms

Hot Flashes,Mood Swings,sleep issues,Weakness,vaginal dryness or discomfort,joint aches.

  • keep a fan or cold water nearby, avoid spicy foods
  • regular sleep and light exercise can help stabilize mood
  • consult a doctor if dryness remains
  • gentle stretching and staying hydrated often eases stiffness

Self Care

prioritize your health and maintain your mental peace

  • this phase can be physically taxing,so don't feel guilty about slowing down
  • talking to friends,family or support groups who understand this phase helps a lot emotionally
  • noting patterns helps you and your doctor find what works
  • Be patient with your body,take deep breaths,medidate.

WHEN TO SEE A DOCTOR!

  • Very heavy or irregular bleeding
  • Severe mood changes,anxiety or depression that affect daily life
  • Symptoms that suddenly worsen or feel unusal
  • Bone density concerns
